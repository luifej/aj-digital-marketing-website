Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we protect your information.

Subscribe to our newsletter or updates

Become a client and provide business information
Opt in to receive SMS messages

This information may include:

Name and contact information (email, phone number, address)
Business information (company name, website, industry)
Marketing preferences and consent records (Transactional and/or Promotional SMS)
Communication history and project details

Information Automatically Collected

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information, including:

IP address and general location information
Browser type and version
Device information (type, operating system)
Pages visited and time spent on our site
Referring website information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

Service Delivery

Providing and managing our digital marketing services
Communicating about projects and deliverables
Processing payments and managing accounts
Providing customer support and technical assistance

Business Operations

Responding to inquiries and consultation requests
Improving our website and services
Analyzing website usage and performance
Sending relevant marketing communications (with your consent)

SMS Messaging (A2P Compliance)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in:

Website hosting and maintenance
Email and SMS delivery services
Analytics and performance monitoring

All third-party service providers are required to maintain the confidentiality and security of your information and are prohibited from using it for any purpose other than providing services to us.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or if we believe such disclosure is necessary to:

Comply with legal processes or government requests

Protect the rights, property, or safety of our clients or others

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against:

Unauthorized access, use, or disclosure
Accidental loss or destruction
Malicious attacks and data breaches

Our security measures include:

Secure Socket Layer (SSL) encryption for data transmission
Regular security assessments and updates
Limited access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
Secure storage and backup procedures

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to:

Fulfill the purposes for which it was collected
Comply with legal, accounting, or reporting requirements
Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

Generally, we retain client information for up to 7 years after the completion of services, unless a longer retention period is required by law. SMS consent records are retained for as long as you are subscribed and up to 4 years after you opt out, for compliance purposes.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal requirements)
Portability: Request transfer of your information to another service provider
Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
Restriction: Request limitation on how we process your information

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.
