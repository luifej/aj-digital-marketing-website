## 2. Trust Building (Social Proof)

**Headline:** Real Results, Not Empty Promises

**Case 1: Delivery and Payroll System**

Before, every delivery was logged on paper, and someone in the office had to manually enter each one into the payroll system, one by one. We built an app where the driver scans the invoice at the moment of delivery, and everything flows straight into a dashboard. That eliminated manual delivery entry entirely, and since every invoice is backed up, a lost physical copy can now be recovered in seconds instead of dug up from paperwork.

Before, checking each Purchase Order against invoices took days of manual searching, one by one. We built a tool that automatically pulls and organizes POs from the relevant portal, sorted by status (pending, in progress, done). That cut the process down from days to just 1 to 2 days.

## Future Product Line — Voice AI (not active yet, do not add to site)

- **Not to be added to site copy** until the feature is actually active and GHL is reactivated. Promising it now would repeat the exact mistake this whole redesign has worked to avoid (claiming capabilities without proof).
